Welcome to HEXA Master Ultra — a vibrant, smooth 3D hexagonal stacking & color-merge puzzle game designed for quick fun and deep strategy!

KEY FEATURES:
• Effortless Offset Drag & Magnetic Snap: Drag tile stacks cleanly above your fingertip with zero finger occlusion and feel tiles magnetically snap onto the hex board!
• Explosive Chain Combos & Fireworks: Stack 10+ matching tiles to trigger dazzling sparks, shockwaves, fireworks, and screen-shake effects.
• Three Exciting Game Modes:
  1. Classic Levels — Handcrafted stages with Coins, Robots, Honeycombs, Stars, Lightning & Cake obstacles.
  2. Time Attack (90s Rush) — Beat the clock! Every cleared stack grants bonus seconds.
  3. Endless Mode — Unlimited puzzle waves with escalating challenge and high-score chasing.
• Daily Missions & Profile Trophies: Complete rotating daily tasks to earn Coins & Gems and unlock Bronze to Diamond milestone badges.
• Cloud Save & Global Leaderboard: Sign in with Google to safeguard your progress across devices and compete globally across Career Score, Top Level, Time Attack & Endless rankings.
• Helpful Boosters & Accessibility: Undo, Shuffle, Smart Hint, Hammer Smash, Pinwheel Storm, Low-Effects mode, and full Colorblind Numerical Symbol mode.
